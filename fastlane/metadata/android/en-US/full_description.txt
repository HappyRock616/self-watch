<b>Calorie counter + fitness tracker + watch sync, in one private app.</b>

Tracking your health usually takes three apps: the watch maker's app for steps and sleep, a calorie counter for food, and a health dashboard to join them up. Three accounts, three clouds.

Self Watch merges them into one. It connects directly to your Garmin watch over Bluetooth, keeps your food diary, and puts calories in and calories out side by side, all on your phone. Your data, connected for you, by you, locally.

<b>Your body. Your data. Your business.</b>

No account. No cloud. No ads. No analytics. No Google Play Services.

<b>Goals at a glance</b>
• Body today: calories eaten and burned, water, energy level, sleep and weight against your goals
• Activity goals: steps, active minutes, workouts and active days this week
• Cards in two sizes that you add, remove and move on any tab

<b>Body and sleep</b>
• Sleep stages, HRV, resting heart rate, stress, readiness and blood oxygen
• Weight with a trend line, body composition, measurements and blood pressure
• Weight goal: safe daily calories to reach your goal weight by your date

<b>Activity and workouts</b>
• Steps, active minutes, VO2 max, weekly effort and heart-rate recovery
• Every workout with route, heart rate, pace and elevation
• Trends for each sport, and trim a workout when you forgot to stop the watch
• Export as GPX, TCX or FIT

<b>Food diary</b>
• Offline food list of about 266,000 foods, packaged products and restaurant meals
• Search that finds the everyday food first, and barcode scanning (no pictures saved)
• Daily balance: calories, protein, carbs, fat, fat types, fiber, sodium and added sugar
• Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and allergen tags, with a warning when a food clashes with your diet
• Tap a meal for its nutrition summary; recent foods for each meal in one tap
• Custom foods, saved meals and recipes

<b>Watch and devices</b>
• Pairs directly with Garmin watches. No Garmin account needed
• Heart-rate straps and scales alongside your watch; a strap always wins for heart rate
• Phone notifications on the watch, with a switch per app
• Weather and GPS help for the watch
• Phone apps can talk to Connect IQ watch apps, with your permission

<b>Import and export</b>
• Bring your history from Gadgetbridge, Garmin Connect exports, FIT/GPX/TCX files, calorie-counting apps and recipe managers
• Export to CSV, JSON, GPX, TCX and FIT
• Full backups, optionally encrypted, to a folder you choose
• Optional Health Connect sync (Android 14+)

<b>Privacy center</b>
Every internet feature has its own switch, and every request is listed: when, where and how much. Only the food list download, GPS help for the watch and your watch apps' own web requests are on at the start; barcode lookups and everything else stay off until you turn them on.

Tested with the vívoactive 5; other current Garmin watches are expected to work, and the app tells you when a model hasn't been verified yet.
