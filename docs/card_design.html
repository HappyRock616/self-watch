Self Watch card design

A new standard for every data card, inspired by Gadgetbridge's dashboard gauges and charts and by Pulse's Today screen. Each card is one picture with the number on it. Text is cut to a title and a few words. Your overlay charts stay exactly as they are and can now sit on the main tabs. Approve or comment on each section; nothing changes in the app until you approve.

The standard

  • One picture per cardA ring, gauge, range bar, week bars, day strip, split bar or trend line. The picture is the card.
  • The number lives on the pictureInside the ring or gauge, or at the end of the line. One big number, its unit, nothing else.
  • Four words, no sentencesTitle plus at most one short tag ("Low", "7-day avg 62"). No explanations on the card; those move to the detail screen.
  • Color is the meaningGarmin's colors for levels: stress blue to orange, sleep stages blue and purple, goal met teal, short of goal yellow.
  • Every chart is readableWeek bars show day letters and a goal line. Day strips show 6, 12, 18. No unlabeled lines.
  • Small cards: number and picture onlyA mini ring, mini gauge or mini bars, with the title. Nothing else.
  • Tap for the detailEvery card opens its full chart screen. Explanations and history live there.
  • Chart cards on the tabsThe overlay charts with toggle chips (Sleep, Heart, Activity, Weight) can be placed on Body or Activity as big cards. Unchanged.
